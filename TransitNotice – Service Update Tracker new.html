About & data limitations


TransitNotice is designed to show only advisories collected from official, permitted sources, with a last-verified time on each. If a source can’t be checked, notices are marked stale rather than treated as current.


This page runs in DEMO mode with sample data. AI answers can contain mistakes: always confirm with the agency’s original notice before travelling.
